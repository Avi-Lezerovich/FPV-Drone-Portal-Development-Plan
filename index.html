Currency and import tax

Why the VAT-free threshold is $75

Since 2 June 2026 personal imports are VAT-free only up to $75 per shipment; above that you pay 18% VAT (ICL Global, 2 Jun 2026 ↗, VATupdate ↗). It had been raised to $150 in Dec 2025 and $130 in Feb 2026 (Times of Israel ↗), so older guides quote those numbers. The test is the value of each shipment (shipping and insurance excluded), not each part: several parts from one seller can arrive as one taxable parcel. The rule has changed three times in a year, so check the Israel Tax Authority before a big order.

        05
        
Wiring and UART map

        
Pick a build, then solder the connections in order. Each card lists every wire, pad to pad. Read the three rules first.

      


      
      

        

          
Power the O4 Pro from 9 V or VBAT, never 5 V

          
It needs 7.4–26.4 V. On a 5 V pad it browns out and reboots mid-flight.

        


        

          
Remove the DJI SBUS wire when using ELRS

          
Lift the tab on the 6-pin plug and pull the yellow SBUS wire, so it can't clash with ELRS on UART2.

        


        

          
Cross TX and RX; LEDs run on 5 V

          
Peripheral TX goes to FC RX, RX to TX. Wire WS2812B LEDs to 5V, GND and LED, arrow pointing away from the pad.

        •

                Open http://10.0.0.1 → Enter UID Phrase & Save
              
          • 
              
        •

                WiFi Connectivity → Enable WiFi → http://10.0.0.1
              
          • 
              
        • 
                4. Recommended ELRS Lua Link Profile
                250 Hz (or 500 Hz) · telem 1:16 · switch: Wide
              
          • 
              
        •

                Dynamic power on · max 250–500 mW

        5. Programming addressable LEDs with "Wiring Order Mode"
        
                  
          
        
          
        
            
        Enable LED_STRIP, open the LED Strip tab and map the 20 LEDs: 0–9 on the left duct, 10–19 on the right.
        

            
        
              
        
                
        1. Click “Wiring Order Mode”
        
                
        
                  Click the Wiring Order Mode button on the right side of the Betaflight LED Strip tab (the grid cursor turns green).
                
        
              
        
              
        
                
        2. Click the grid squares in order, 0 → 19
        
                
        
                  Click 20 squares on the grid matching your physical wire path: start at the LED closest to the FC solder pad (0) through the Left Duct (0–9) and across to the Right Duct (10–19).
                
        
              
        
              
        
                
        3. Leave wiring mode and assign functions
        
                
        
                  Turn Wiring Order Mode OFF. Drag-select LEDs 0–9 or 10–19, set Function: Color (or Arm State), check Warning: Battery, pick a color swatch, and click Save!
